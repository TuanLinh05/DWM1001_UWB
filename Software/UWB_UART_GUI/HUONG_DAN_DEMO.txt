DWM1001 UWB GROUND CONTROL - GOI DEMO PORTABLE
================================================

1. May demo khong can cai Python.
2. Double-click DWM1001_UWB_Ground_Control.exe de ket noi UART that.
3. Double-click CHAY_DEMO.cmd de chay telemetry mo phong khong can board.
4. Khi dung UART that: bam Lam moi, chon dung COM. Baud: 1000000 (mac dinh)
   cho Tag_DevKit; 115200 cho Tag PCB noi USB-UART; qua gateway ESP32-C3 thi
   baud nao cung duoc.
5. Thu muc data_logs va file anchor_layout.json se duoc tao canh file EXE.

Ban build: 1.3.0 - Windows x64

Tab RANGE_MEAS 1.3.0:
- Moi phep do mot dong ban ghi: Hz that cua tung anchor, ban ghi mat,
  nhieu, FP/RX, chi bao NLOS (RX - FP), clock offset va thoi gian slot.
- Dong do = NLOS >= 10 dB, cam = >= 6 dB, vang = chua calibration.
- Bieu do tung phep do theo dong ho TAG (khoang cach + NLOS).
- Nut Bat/Tat RANGE_MEAS; GUI tu bat khi UART >= 460800 baud.
- Khi ghi du lieu, them file meas.csv.

Tab Phan tich 1.2.0:
- Chon anchor, nguon, so mau (mac dinh 3000), roi bam Bat dau lay mau.
- Do thi khong chay realtime; chi ve mot lan sau khi capture du mau.
- 4 do thi: chuoi thoi gian, histogram, Allan deviation va Welch PSD.
- Luu bieu do thanh PNG/PDF/SVG, hoac luu tung mau va thong ke thanh CSV.
- Doc dung timebase TAG 50 Hz cho valid va du lieu diagnostic CAL_MISSING.
- Loi phan tich duoc ghi log, khong lam dung vong nhan UART cua GUI.

Luu y:
- Khong dua TTL 5 V vao DWM1001C.
- May khac can dung Windows x64.
- Neu Windows SmartScreen canh bao do file chua co chu ky so, chon chi khi ban
  xac nhan file duoc chep truc tiep tu project nay.
